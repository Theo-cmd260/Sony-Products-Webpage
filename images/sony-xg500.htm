Powerful Party Sound anywhere

You've got to go wherever the party takes you. That means that you need a speaker that can deliver a big sound wherever you go. With the XG500, you'll take a Powerful Party Sound with you, and with built-in lighting you can take a little atmosphere too.

Built to last

The XG500 is built to last. It's got a durable exterior, it's IP66 Water resistant and Dustproof, and it's got plenty of battery life. So, the only question is: where do you want to take it?

...
Take the party outside

With IP66 Water-resistant and Dustproof rating, this speaker is ready for just about anything. That means that you can listen by the pool, at the park, or even at the beach.

Party for longer than ever 

With a huge 30 hours of battery when fully charged, the XG500 speaker will play and play, so you can keep on partying without worrying about the tunes suddenly stopping.

Charge up in a hurry

If you find yourself a little short of time, don't worry. Quick charging will give you three hours of play on just a 10-minute charge.

Take care of your battery

Battery care feature helps you avoid overcharging and will keep your battery in optimal condition for longer.

...
A handy handle for easy portability

Want to listen on the move? No problem. With a comfortable handle, the XG500 is made for taking with you wherever you want to go.

...
Get out and get loud

The XG500 brings together the X-Balanced Speaker Unit, High-efficiency Tweeter, and passive radiators, to give you a powerful sound that's perfect for outdoor parties.

...
X-Balanced Speaker Unit 

The innovative X-Balanced Speaker Unit has a non-circular diaphragm with more area than conventional speakers. That means you'll get a richer, clearer sound, no matter what style of music you're listening to.

...
Bigger speaker, better sound

The maximized diaphragm area increases sound pressure for a deeper and punchier bass sound, clearer vocals, and an altogether enhanced listening experience. It also reduces driver excursion while maintaining the same sound pressure, resulting in less distortion and greater vocal clarity.

  • Bigger speaker, better sound

Sound worth sharing

The technology in the XG500 works in harmony for better sound quality. The X-Balanced Speaker Unit gives deep, punchy bass and vocal clarity even with loud sound. A High-efficiency Tweeter provides crisp and spacious sound for high frequencies. Passive radiators optimize to reproduce clear bass sound. Put it all together and you get a great sound worth sharing with your friends.

Give the bass a boost

MEGA BASS allows you to really dial up the bass. So if you're listening to something with a beat, you'll get a deep and punchy bass sound. 

Hear it like it's live

There's something special about the sound of live music. With LIVE SOUND you can recreate that unique atmosphere and re-live your favorite live music experiences over and over again.

Let there be light

All-new ambient illumination lighting produces modern and subtle lighting that's perfect for parties or for everyday use. You can select a variety of different lighting patterns from the Sony | Music Center app.

...
Sing your heart out

If you're the next singing superstar, you can connect a mic to your speaker and sing your heart out, or plug in your guitar and use the speaker as an amp.

Make the most of your music

The XG500 is designed to work around your life. So, whether you want to connect wirelessly or plug in, the choice is entirely up to you.

Specifications and Features

The SRS-XG500 is perfect for taking a powerful sound outside. The combination of the X-Balanced Speaker Unit, High-efficiency Tweeter, and passive radiators delivers a crisp and clear sound whether you're listening in an indoor space or outside. Plus, with a durable exterior, an IP66 water resistant and dustproof rating, 30-hour battery life, and an easy-to-hold handle, you can take it almost wherever you want to go.

  • IP66 water resistant and dustproof

  • 30 hours of battery life and quick charging

  • Handle for comfortable portability

  • Powerful Party Sound

  • X-Balanced Speaker Unit
